The GameMaster's Apprentice — local deck app

This app implements the 14 randomizer fields of a GMA card deck and runs them off your local cards.js file. The card content itself is yours — populate the file from your PnP using app/extract/extract.py (see cards-howto.md), or hand-edit it.

Two ways to ask the oracle

  • Draw a card (top right): one card flips and every randomizer field updates at once. Read whichever field answers your current question.
  • Per-field draw (the small "draw" button in each cell): flips just that randomizer. The rest of the card view updates too — different randomizers on the same card aren't independent.

The 14 randomizers

  1. Difficulty — 1–10 bell curve. Lock picks, reactions, "how strong / friendly / heavy."
  2. Likely Odds — answer to a yes/no question, in three odds tiers (Bad 25%, Even 50%, Good 75%). YES! / NO! are emphatic criticals, printed on certain cards.
  3. Dice — d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, d00/d10 percentile. Pre-rolled values on each card.
  4. Norse Rune — one of 24 Elder Futhark, Tarot-style for symbolic interpretation.
  5. Element — Earth / Fire / Air / Water. Tone modifier on other randomizers.
  6. Random Event — Verb + Adjective + Noun phrase. Decide what it means in scene.
  7. Sensory — Hear / See / Feel / Smell-Taste. Atmosphere prompts.
  8. Tag Symbols — 3 of 10 icons. Map to your own table per scene (NPC types / room features / loot / etc.).
  9. Scatter Die — direction (N/NE/E/SE/S/SW/W/NW) + hit/miss. Spatial randomizer.
  10. Belongings — category + 3 examples. NPC pockets / loot.
  11. Names — 3 names, often male / female / neutral.
  12. Catalyst — a one-line story incident to start a scene.
  13. Location — adventure setting prompt.
  14. Virtue & Vice — NPC motivation pair.

Tension

Each time you draw a card or rate a question, Tension goes up by 1. When it hits the threshold (default 11), a Random Event auto-fires and Tension resets to 0. That's the GMA "every-eleven-questions surprise" loop. Adjust manually with the + / − buttons — raise it after a frantic scene, lower it after a quiet one.

Threads & Characters (Lists)

Cards have no memory of previous state — but the app does. Click Lists to manage two persistent rosters: Threads (active plot threads) and Characters (NPCs you've met). When Tension auto-fires a Random Event, the app picks a random active entry from these lists and surfaces it in the banner — so the surprise anchors to story you've already established instead of a brand-new stranger every time.

Quick NPC

Click Quick NPC to spin a complete NPC from the current deck + active genre pack: name, reaction (1-of-5: Hostile / Wary / Neutral / Friendly / Helpful), virtue, vice, belongings, element. Reroll until one feels right; then Save to Characters to add to your roster.

State

Tension, last-drawn card, genre, threads, and characters all save to localStorage under oracle.gma.v2. Older oracle.gma.v1 data is migrated automatically on first load.
