rpgGenerator

— two card-based oracle apps for solo RPG play

Pick your app

Both apps run entirely in the browser, save state to localStorage, and need no server. Open either tile to start.

Universal RPG Oracle

GMA-style oracle with six genre packs (Fantasy, Post-Apoc, Cyberpunk, Weird Horror, Steampunk, Sci-Fi). Yes/no with odds, NPC reactions, virtue + vice, catalyst hooks, sensory snippets, names & locations. No card data needed — content ships in the genre packs.

Use when: you want a self-contained oracle that runs out of the box across six genres.

GMA Local Deck

Front-end for a personal copy of the GameMaster's Apprentice deck. All 14 randomizers + Tension counter + Threads/Characters lists + Quick NPC generator. Ships with an empty cards.js — populate from your own PnP using the included extract.py + merge.py (see Gamemaster/app/cards-howto.md).

Use when: you own the GMA PnP and want a digital local front-end.
Universal RPG Oracle is original work (mechanics inspired by GameMaster's Apprentice and Mythic GME). GMA Local Deck is a front-end only — card content is never bundled; you supply your own legally-owned deck data.
